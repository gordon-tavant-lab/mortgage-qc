B3-3.4-14, Section 8 Housing Choice Voucher Homeownership
Program Payments (03/04/2026)
The Housing Choice Voucher Homeownership Program (more commonly known as Section 8) is an acceptable
source of qualifying income.
Criteria Requirements
Documentation
The lender must obtain evidence:
• of the payment amount from the public agency that issues the
voucher, and
• the borrower will receive at least one payment on or before the ﬁrst
payment due date. 
Income History No minimum history is required.
Income Continuance The lender is not required to verify continuance unless they have
reason to believe the income may not continue.
Determination of Qualifying
Income
The lender must determine the monthly payment amount from the
public agency that issues the voucher. 
Note: The full amount of documented qualifying income is nontaxable
and may be grossed up. See B3-3.1-01, General Income Information
 for nontaxable income guidelines.
